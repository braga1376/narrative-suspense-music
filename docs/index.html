Narrative Suspense as Musical Tension

Composing Film Music From Video

Francisco Braga, Nuno Correia, Roger B. Dannenberg, Gilberto Bernardes

ACM Multimedia 2026 · Rio de Janeiro

Paper (DOI) Code

What this is

The system extracts narrative structure from a film — characters, story threads, events — and computes a suspense value for each event. It then composes a music whose musical tension is aligned to that suspense curve through multi-objective evolutionary optimization.

The excerpts below are the three used in the listening study reported in the paper. Each is scored by the full system. All output is piano, rendered from MIDI with a single instrument; the paper's claim concerns the alignment between music and narrative, not the realism of the rendering.

The suspense trace under each clip is the curve the score was aligned to, plotted over the excerpt's events.

Metropolis

1927 · Fritz Lang · events 21–24 · ~143 s

The aftermath of the machine explosion and Freder's confrontation with his father. The strongest alignment effect of the three: explicit stakes, a visible turning point, and a shared expectation of where the scene is going.

Suspense · events 21–24

Easy Street

1917 · Charles Chaplin · public domain · events 34–40 · ~117 s

The Bully intimidates the Tramp, and the turning point where the Tramp defeats him. A moderate alignment effect: the stakes are legible, but the comic register leaves some room for interpretation.

Suspense · events 34–40

Spring

2019 · Blender Studio · CC BY 4.0 · events 4–7 · ~118 s

A journey into a frozen forest and the appearance of the stone giants. In the study, this excerpt showed no significant effect — the narrative is the most ambiguous of the three.

Suspense · events 4–7

Repository contents

The repository holds the full pipeline — narrative extraction, the adapted suspense model, the harmony grammar, tension measurement, and the NSGA-II optimization — together with the extracted narrative structures and the generated MIDI for every excerpt, so the composition stage can be re-run without access to the extraction model.

Narrative structures are in data/narratives/, generated scores in output/scores/.
